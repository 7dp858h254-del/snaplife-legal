SnapLife Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

SnapLife (the “app”) is a personal assistant for style, beauty, meals and everyday plans, developed and operated by Ghadim Developer, a Canadian technology company (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have. We designed SnapLife so that most of what you create stays on your own device.

Questions or requests: support@ghadimdeveloper.com. This policy covers the SnapLife app only; the website at ghadimdeveloper.com has its own notice.

1. Information that stays on your device

Your wardrobe items, outfits, beauty products, recipes, grocery lists, agenda entries, reminders, notes, appearance choices and the photos you attach are stored on your device. We do not receive them and we cannot see them. If you delete the app, or choose to delete items, that data is removed from the device. Backup files you create with “Backup & restore” are saved where you choose; keep them safe, because they are not encrypted by us and anyone with the file can read them.

2. Information we collect when you create an account

An account is optional. If you create one we collect:

  • Email address, to confirm your account, sign you in, send confirmation and password-reset messages and answer support requests.
  • Your name (as you enter it) and a self-reported country, to personalise the app and show options that apply where you live.
  • Your password, which is processed by our authentication provider and stored only as a secure hash. We never see or store it in readable form.
  • Technical records that the authentication provider keeps to run and secure sign-in (for example sign-in times and network addresses).

If you turn on Face ID or fingerprint sign-in, your device’s secure storage holds a sign-in token. Your biometric data is handled by your device’s operating system and never reaches us.

3. Optional features and what they send

  • Weather and location. If you allow location or choose a Canadian city, the app requests current weather from Environment and Climate Change Canada (api.weather.gc.ca) and, to find a city, a Government of Canada geolocation service (geolocator.api.geo.ca). These requests contain the city or approximate coordinates and, as with any web request, your network address. You can turn weather off in Settings. We do not store your location.
  • Reminders and notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Notifications are generated locally, not by our servers.
  • AI assistant (limited testing). Where the AI assistant is available, the text you type is sent through our server to our AI provider, OpenAI, to produce a reply. We configure requests so that the content is not stored by the provider for later use, and we do not save your conversation in the app after you leave the screen. The app does not automatically attach your wardrobe, photos or plans; only what you type is sent. The one exception is “Style me from my wardrobe”, which works only after you agree on screen: it then sends the names, categories and short notes of up to 40 of your wardrobe pieces (never photos) together with the occasion you type, so the assistant can suggest outfits from what you own. You can withdraw that agreement at any time in the same screen; the reply is shown there and not saved. Do not enter passwords, sensitive health information or information about children. We keep a usage record (your account identifier, counts and cost) to enforce limits.
  • Voice notes (limited testing). Where voice is available, you tap the microphone, record up to 30 seconds and the recording is sent through our server to OpenAI to be turned into text, which appears on your screen for you to review. The microphone is used only while you are recording. We do not store the audio; we keep a usage record (your account identifier and cost) to enforce limits. Do not speak passwords, sensitive health details or information about children.
  • Voice rooms (limited testing). Where voice rooms are available, you can start or join a private room with an invite code. Audio is carried live by our real-time audio provider, LiveKit, and is not recorded or stored by SnapLife. People in the room see your display name and hear you while your microphone is on; the microphone is used only inside a room. If you choose to share a reminder, recipe, outfit or photo, it goes directly to the people in that room through the same live connection and is not saved by SnapLife. We keep a record of each room (its name, the account that created it, who joined and when) so we can run the service, enforce room limits and handle reports. Rooms last up to 12 hours and hold up to 6 people; the person who started a room can remove people or end it.
  • Public information. Where public directories are shown, they come from public government sources.
  • Photos. Photos you pick are used on your device only, with one opt-in exception: “AI manicure preview”. After you agree on screen and tap Create preview, a small copy of your hand photo (shrunk to about 1,000 pixels wide) and your nail choices are sent through our server to OpenAI to create an illustration of the manicure, which is shown on your screen. Your original photo is not changed or uploaded, and neither the photo copy nor the preview is stored by SnapLife. You can withdraw your agreement at any time. Only use photos you have the right to use, and do not include other people’s faces or sensitive content.

4. Service providers who handle data for us

  • Supabase — hosts account authentication and our database.
  • Resend — delivers the emails we send you (confirmation codes, password resets).
  • LiveKit — carries live audio and shared items in voice rooms.
  • OpenAI — processes AI assistant text where that feature is enabled.
  • Apple and Google — distribute the app (including Apple’s TestFlight) under their own policies.

These providers may process data in the United States or other countries, where local laws may differ from Canadian law. We do not sell your personal information, do not use it for advertising, and do not use advertising or cross-app tracking identifiers.

5. How long we keep information

Account information is kept while your account is open. AI usage records are kept only as needed to apply usage limits and for accounting. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify the account information, except records we must keep to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete account), or by emailing us.
  • Access or correct the information we hold about you, or withdraw consent, by emailing us. We respond within 30 days.
  • Export your data on your device with Backup & restore.
  • Turn off location, notifications or Face ID in Settings or in your device’s system settings.
  • If you are in Canada and are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial regulator.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls on our servers, hashed passwords and device-level secure storage for sign-in tokens. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Children

SnapLife is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Emails

We send only account messages you request or need (confirmation code, password reset, security notices) and replies to your questions. We do not send marketing email without your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app. The current version is always at this address.
